What this app does

Zero2AIBuilder Publisher is a personal publishing tool built by Ahmed Saeed (Zero2AIBuilder). It lets the account owner post their own short videos to their own TikTok account (@zero2aibulider) through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Data we collect

  • TikTok login data (Login Kit): your basic TikTok profile info (username, display name, profile picture) — used only to confirm your identity when you authorize the app.
  • Videos you choose to publish: video files you select are uploaded to TikTok at your direction.

How we use it

Your data is used solely to log you in and publish videos you explicitly choose to publish. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We run no advertising and no analytics on your data.

Data storage

Authorization tokens are stored securely and used only to act on your behalf when you publish. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your TikTok settings (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions), which immediately stops all access.

Children

This tool is not intended for children under 13 and is operated by a single adult account owner.
